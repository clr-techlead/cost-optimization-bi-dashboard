# Preliminary Insights

This document summarizes the first findings from the synthetic expense dataset. The figures are intended to demonstrate the analytical workflow and should be replaced with validated business data before operational use.

| Over-budget transactions | 13 of 24 |

## Initial findings

### 1. IT is the largest cost pressure

IT represents the highest actual spend at $95,800 and the largest department-level variance at $6,300 over budget. This makes IT the first area to investigate for contract reviews, cloud usage optimization, and hardware purchasing controls.

### 2. CloudCore is the main supplier driver

CloudCore contributes the largest supplier variance at $6,350 over budget. A supplier review should examine recurring commitments, price changes, usage-based billing, and opportunities to consolidate services.

### 3. Operations has a meaningful recurring-cost gap

Operations is $4,050 over budget. The dashboard should allow users to drill from department to category, supplier, and transaction to determine whether the gap is structural or caused by isolated events.

### 4. Budget compliance requires attention

13 of 24 transactions are over budget, indicating that the issue is not limited to a single transaction. A useful next step is to compare compliance by month, cost type, department, and supplier.

## Recommended business actions

1. Prioritize an IT and CloudCore spend review.
2. Separate recurring and variable costs to identify controllable commitments.
3. Add monthly trend analysis and variance alerts.
4. Validate the synthetic findings against ERP, procurement, or accounting data.
5. Define an owner and target date for each savings opportunity.

## Limitations

- The dataset is synthetic and contains only 24 transactions.
- No baseline period or external benchmark is available.
- Savings opportunity is a screening estimate, not a confirmed financial benefit.
- Actual decisions require source-system validation and stakeholder review.
